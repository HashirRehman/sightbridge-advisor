# Sightbridge: where AI creates leverage first

*Hashir · AI Engineering Lead take-home · public sources only. **Fact** = from public sources; **View** = my recommendation.*

## 1. What Sightbridge does, and for whom

**Fact.** Sightbridge Capital Partners (Santa Monica) launched publicly in June 2026 as an "institutional real estate platform built around tax-advantaged programs" for the U.S. private wealth channel. It is starting with 1031 Delaware Statutory Trust (DST) programs, including a DST-to-721 UPREIT path; Qualified Opportunity Zone strategies are expected to follow. Programs are built with institutional owner-operators and distributed through financial advisors to investors deferring capital gains.

The leaders come from institutional real estate distribution:

- **Jay Frank (CEO):** formerly President/COO/Head of Distribution at Cantor Fitzgerald Asset Management.
- **Steve Rokoszewski (COO):** founded Anchor Advisory Services, acquired by Praxis in 2024.
- **Kamil Poltorak (Real Estate):** formerly at Cantor; helped structure 40+ offerings.

In September 2026 it hired two Managing Directors of Private Wealth, ahead of its first programs.

**View.** Sightbridge is a sponsor and distributor whose product is trust. Programs are complex, illiquid and sold through intermediaries, so speed and accuracy of information decide shelf space.

## 2. Who it needs to win over

1. **Broker-dealer and RIA home offices and due-diligence teams.** They approve products onto platforms. Without selling agreements there is no distribution. *(Fact: one MD covers "national accounts, platform relationships and home office due diligence".)*
2. **Financial advisors.** They choose between competing DST sponsors for clients facing 45/180-day 1031 deadlines. They need fast, correct, compliant answers.
3. **Platforms and third-party due-diligence providers.** Their reports and listings shape advisor perception at scale.
4. **Institutional owner-operators and lenders.** They supply the properties and must trust Sightbridge's execution.
5. **Adjacent influencers.** Qualified intermediaries, CPAs and estate attorneys sit at the moment a gain is realised.

## 3. Where AI creates the most leverage in 90 days

**View.** Sightbridge's constraint is a small distribution team facing a large number of advisors and home offices, while answering the same questions from first principles. Leverage comes from making approved knowledge instantly reusable, never from AI giving advice.

**Build first: a cited, compliance-reviewed knowledge assistant over approved materials.** Advisor Q&A and due-diligence questionnaire (DDQ) drafting share one engine. This take-home is its skeleton: answers only from approved sources, verified citations, deterministic refusals of personalised advice, and an audit log for supervision.

| Days | Deliverable | Success measure |
|---|---|---|
| 0–30 | Load approved PPMs, offering docs and FAQs; internal-only assistant for the private wealth and operations team; 50-question eval set signed off by compliance | ≥90% of eval answers correct and cited; zero uncited claims |
| 31–60 | DDQ drafting: first-draft answers from the same library, always reviewed by a human; gap report of questions the library can't answer | DDQ turnaround time halved; gaps closed weekly |
| 61–90 | Pilot with 10–20 advisors through wholesalers; weekly review of audit log and refusals | Repeat usage; fewer repetitive inbound questions; no compliance exceptions |

**Not first:** personalised suitability, investor-facing chat or autonomous outreach. All carry FINRA/SEC communications risk that outweighs early value.
